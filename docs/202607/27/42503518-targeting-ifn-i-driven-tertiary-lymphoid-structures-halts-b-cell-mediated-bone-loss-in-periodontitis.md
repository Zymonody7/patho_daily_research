## 摘要
淋巴细胞在适应性免疫中发挥核心作用，是牙周炎中RANKL的主要来源。因此，了解它们在牙龈内的组织和激活至关重要。本研究探讨了三级淋巴结构（TLSs）的作用，这是一种协调局部免疫反应的异位淋巴聚集体。通过多重免疫组化染色，我们发现TLSs随着炎症严重程度的增加而形成并成熟。这些结构作为B细胞激活的中心，并与较差的手术预后相关。鉴于成纤维细胞在启动TLS组装中的已知作用，我们探索了其潜在机制。利用单细胞RNA测序和体外实验，我们发现口腔病原体激活了牙龈成纤维细胞中的IFN-I-IRF7轴，导致IFNβ过量产生，并驱动其分化为产生CXCL13的亚型，从而促进TLS的组织。此外，在小鼠牙周炎模型中，我们证明了TLS内激活的IgD+CD80+ B细胞通过分泌RANKL促进破骨细胞生成和骨吸收。在治疗方面，抑制IFN-I信号传导通过抑制TLS形成及随后的RANKL产生，阻断了这一致病级联反应，从而减轻了牙周炎的骨丢失。总之，我们的研究结果确立了TLS是协调牙周炎中B细胞介导的骨破坏的病理中心，并强调了IFN-I信号通路是具有前景的治疗干预靶点。

## Abstract
Lymphocytes play a central role in adaptive immunity and represent the primary source of RANKL in periodontitis. Understanding their organization and activation within gingiva is therefore essential. This study investigates the role of tertiary lymphoid structures (TLSs), ectopic lymphoid aggregates that orchestrate local immune responses. Using multiplex immunohistochemistry staining, we found that TLSs form and mature with increasing inflammatory severity. These structures function as hubs for B cell activation and were associated with poorer surgical outcomes. Given the known role of fibroblasts in initiating TLS assembly, we explored the underlying mechanism. Using single-cell RNA sequencing and in vitro assays, we identified that oral pathogens activate the IFN-I-IRF7 axis in gingival fibroblasts, leading to overproduction of IFNβ and driving their differentiation into a CXCL13-producing subtype that facilitates TLS organization. Furthermore, in a mouse periodontitis model, we demonstrated that activated IgD+CD80+ B cells within TLSs promote osteoclastogenesis and bone resorption via RANKL secretion. Therapeutically, inhibition of IFN-I signaling disrupted this pathogenic cascade by suppressing TLS formation and subsequent RANKL production, thereby mitigating periodontitis bone loss. In conclusion, our findings establish TLSs as pathological hubs that coordinate B cell-mediated bone destruction in periodontitis and highlight the IFN-I signaling pathway as a promising target for therapeutic intervention.
