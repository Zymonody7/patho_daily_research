## 摘要
口腔微生物组主要包括位于龈上和龈下菌斑中的微生物。这些细菌群落通过形成复杂的调节网络来维持特定生态位的稳定和宿主免疫稳态，从而维持牙周健康。当这种稳定性被破坏时，会发生局部牙周菌群失调，导致有毒产物的过度产生。宏组学技术的最新进展揭示了口腔微生物组的特征。在本综述中，我们讨论了与牙周病相关的口腔微生物组的最新见解，主要关注细菌成员。此外，还讨论了这些细菌成员在牙周健康和牙周病发展过程中的组成、功能和代谢程序，以及它们与宿主细胞的相互作用。本综述旨在为建立基于微生物组指数的牙周病风险预测、诊断和预后评估模型提供指导。此外，本文分析了菌群失调影响牙周病发生发展的潜在机制，强调了核心微生物组的作用，并为开发促进微生物组稳态的新治疗策略和靶点提供了潜在解决方案。最后，讨论了该领域仍存在的重要问题以及未来研究的关键点。

## Abstract
The oral microbiome, mainly includes microorganisms located in supragingival and subgingival plaques. These bacterial communities maintain the stability of specific niches and host immune homeostasis by forming a complex regulatory network, thereby maintaining periodontal health. Local periodontal dysbiosis occurs when this stability is disrupted, which leads to the excessive production of toxic products. Recent advances in meta-omics technologies have revealed the characters of the oral microbiome. In this review, we discuss the most recent insights into the oral microbiome associated with periodontal disease, mainly focusing on the bacterial members. The composition, function and metabolic programs of these bacterial members under conditions of periodontal health and periodontal disease development, as well as their interactions with host cells, are also discussed. This review aims to provide guidance for establishing models for periodontal disease risk prediction, diagnosis and prognosis evaluation based on microbiome indices. Additionally, this paper analyses the potential mechanism by which dysbiosis may affect the occurrence and development of periodontal disease, emphasizes the role of the core microbiome, and provides potential solutions for the development of new therapeutic strategies and targets to promote microbiome homeostasis. Finally, the important remaining questions in this field and critical points for future research are discussed.
