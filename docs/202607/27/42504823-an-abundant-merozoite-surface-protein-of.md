## 摘要
疟疾裂殖子表面蛋白（MSPs）被认为在红细胞（RBC）入侵中发挥重要作用，且其在寄生虫表面的暴露使其成为极具吸引力的候选疫苗。然而，它们在入侵中的作用尚未得到直接证实，其生物学功能仍不清楚。恶性疟原虫（Plasmodium falciparum）谱系中保留的一种可能的祖先蛋白 PfMSP2 是丰度最高的裂殖子表面蛋白之一，也是疫苗开发的重点。利用 CRISPR-Cas9 基因编辑技术，我们从两种不同的恶性疟原虫株系中敲除了 PfMSP2，发现其对体外寄生虫的复制或表型没有影响，证明了它对于红细胞入侵并非必不可少。有趣的是，PfMSP2 的缺失增强了针对其他参与入侵的裂殖子蛋白（特别是 PfAMA1）抗体的抑制效力。在固相模型中，PfMSP2 蛋白浓度的增加以剂量依赖的方式降低了针对 PfAMA1 的不同抗体的结合。这些数据表明，PfMSP2 可以调节裂殖子对保护性抑制抗体的敏感性。本研究的结果改变了我们对 PfMSP2 潜在功能的认识，并提出了疟疾研究中的一个新概念，即一种表面蛋白可以降低针对另一种不同抗原的抗体的保护效力。这些发现对于理解疟疾免疫和指导疫苗开发具有重要意义。

## Abstract
Malaria merozoite surface proteins (MSPs) are thought to have important roles in red blood cell (RBC) invasion and their exposure on the parasite surface makes them attractive vaccine candidates. However, their role in invasion has not been directly demonstrated and their biological functions remain unknown. One of the most abundant merozoite surface proteins is PfMSP2, a likely ancestral protein that has been maintained in the Plasmodium falciparum lineage and is a focus of vaccine development. Using CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing, we removed PfMSP2 from two different P. falciparum lines with no impact on parasite replication or phenotype in vitro, demonstrating that it is not essential for RBC invasion. Interestingly, loss of PfMSP2 led to increased inhibitory potency of antibodies targeting other merozoite proteins involved in invasion, particularly PfAMA1. In a solid-phase model, increasing concentrations of PfMSP2 protein reduced binding of different antibodies against PfAMA1 in a dose-dependent manner. These data suggest that PfMSP2 can modulate the susceptibility of merozoites to protective inhibitory antibodies. The results of this study change our understanding of the potential functions of PfMSP2 and establish a new concept in malaria where a surface protein can reduce the protective efficacy of antibodies targeting a different antigen. These findings have important implications for understanding malaria immunity and informing vaccine development.
