## 摘要
过度纤维化和植入物相关感染仍然是伤口愈合和术后组织修复中的主要挑战，通常会导致再生受损和长期并发症。尽管甲基丙烯酰化明胶 (GelMA) 水凝胶因其生物相容性和可调的物理化学性质而广泛应用于再生医学，但它们本质上缺乏抗纤维化和抗菌功能。在此，我们报道了一种双功能 GelMA 基水凝胶 (GelMA-AMP/AFP)，通过共价共偶联抗纤维化肽 (DR8) 和广谱抗菌肽 (P9-4) 实现，从而能够在单一生物材料平台内同时调节纤维化反应和细菌定植。多肽功能化的 GelMA 水凝胶保留了其多孔微观结构、机械完整性和溶胀行为，同时在 14 天内提供了持续的多肽释放曲线。在 TGF-β1 诱导的体外纤维化成纤维细胞模型中，该双功能水凝胶在基因和蛋白质水平上均显著抑制了成纤维细胞的增殖和迁移，减少了胶原沉积，并下调了关键的促纤维化标志物，包括 COL1A1、ACTA2、FN1 和 TGF-β1。与此同时，相同的 GelMA-AMP/AFP 平台在不诱导细胞毒性的情况下，有效抑制了多重耐药病原体（包括耐甲氧西林金黄色葡萄球菌 (MRSA) 和多重耐药铜绿假单胞菌 (MDRPA)）的细菌粘附和生物膜形成。本研究报道了一种基于 GelMA 的生物材料，可同时整合共价偶联的抗纤维化肽和抗菌肽，从而在体外单一材料系统中实现了纤维化的并发抑制和生物膜形成的抑制。通过在不损害材料性能的情况下结合互补的生物功能，本研究提出了一种化学驱动的多功能生物材料设计策略。该策略可能为未来针对先进伤口敷料、术后粘连预防和易感染组织修复的研究提供有用的基础，尚待进一步的体内验证。

## Abstract
Excessive fibrosis and implant-associated infections remain major challenges in wound healing and post-surgical tissue repair, often resulting in impaired regeneration and long-term complications. Although gelatin methacryloyl (GelMA) hydrogels are widely used in regenerative medicine due to their biocompatibility and tunable physicochemical properties, they intrinsically lack antifibrotic and antibacterial functionality. Herein, we report a dual-functional GelMA-based hydrogel (GelMA-AMP/AFP) achieved by the covalent co-conjugation of an antifibrotic peptide (DR8) and a broad-spectrum antimicrobial peptide (P9-4), enabling simultaneous regulation of fibrotic responses and bacterial colonization within a single biomaterial platform. The peptide-functionalized GelMA hydrogels preserved their porous microarchitecture, mechanical integrity, and swelling behavior while providing a sustained peptide release profile over 14 days. In a TGF-β1-induced in vitro fibrotic fibroblast model, the dual-functional hydrogel significantly suppressed fibroblast proliferation and migration, reduced collagen deposition, and downregulated key profibrotic markers, including COL1A1, ACTA2, FN1, and TGF-β1, at both gene and protein levels. In parallel, the same GelMA-AMP/AFP platform effectively inhibited bacterial adhesion and biofilm formation by multidrug-resistant pathogens, including methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA) and multidrug-resistant Pseudomonas aeruginosa (MDRPA), without inducing cytotoxicity. This study reports a GelMA-based biomaterial to simultaneously integrate covalently conjugated antifibrotic and antimicrobial peptides, thereby achieving concurrent suppression of fibrosis and inhibition of biofilm formation within a single material system, in vitro. By combining complementary biological functionalities without compromising material properties, this study presents a chemically driven strategy for multifunctional biomaterial design. This strategy may provide a useful foundation for future studies targeting advanced wound dressings, post-surgical adhesion prevention, and infection-prone tissue repair, pending further in vivo validation.
