## 摘要
背景：衡量人群免疫力对于疫情防范至关重要，但将个体免疫力转化为人群水平免疫力指标的方法仍不成熟。本研究旨在以流感作为模型病原体，开发并评估人群免疫力评估指标。方法：在这项多国回顾性观察研究中，我们分析了来自中国、香港、越南和美国的六项研究中的 41,835 份血清样本，涵盖了 2009 年至 2024 年间的 27 次流感流行。我们根据个体抗体滴度构建了四种人群免疫力评估指标：几何平均滴度、非初次感染个体比例、人群免疫比例，以及利用下一代矩阵框架计算的再生数相对降幅。我们评估了这些指标预测哪种亚型（H1N1 与 H3N2）将占主导地位的能力，预测疫情规模是否会比上一季节更大或更小的能力，并分析了其与香港一个跨越八个流感季节（包含流行期内血清采集）的纵向队列中后续累积发病率的相关性。结果：这些指标相对于前一季节的亚型特异性变化正确预测了 57-86% 的 H1N1 主导季节和 100% 的 H3N2 主导季节，受试者工作特征曲线下面积（AUROC）为 79-93%。这些指标在 67-83% 的案例中正确预测了更大规模的疫情，在 90-100% 的案例中正确预测了更小规模的疫情，AUROC 为 83-92%。预测性能因评估指标和流感亚型而异，部分评估值的置信区间较宽，表明精度适中。在香港纵向队列中，所有四种指标均与 H1N1 随后的 30 天累积发病率呈负相关（Pearson 相关系数为 -0.23 至 -0.46），而 H3N2 的相关性大多不显著。解释：我们开发并验证了四种基于个体抗体滴度的互补性人群免疫力评估指标，这些指标在不同环境下对流感亚型主导地位和疫情规模趋势显示出预测效用。这些评估指标可为季节性流感防范、监测优先级确定和医疗资源规划提供参考。资助：香港医务卫生局和研究资助局。

## Abstract
BACKGROUND: Measuring population immunity is crucial for epidemic preparedness, but methods to translate individual immunity into population-level immunity metrics remain underdeveloped. We aimed to develop and evaluate population immunity estimators using influenza as a model pathogen. METHODS: In this multicountry, retrospective observational study, we analysed 41 835 serum samples from six studies in China, Hong Kong, Viet Nam, and the USA across 27 influenza epidemics in 2009-24. We constructed four population-immunity estimators from individual antibody titres: geometric mean titre, proportion of non-naive individuals, proportion of population immune, and relative reduction in reproductive number calculated using a next-generation matrix framework. We evaluated the ability of these estimators to predict which subtype (H1N1 vs H3N2) would dominate, to predict whether epidemics would be larger or smaller than the previous season, and to correlate with subsequent cumulative incidence in a longitudinal Hong Kong cohort spanning eight influenza seasons with within-epidemic serum collection. FINDINGS: Subtype-specific relative changes in these estimators from previous seasons correctly predicted 57-86% of H1N1-dominant seasons and 100% of H3N2-dominant seasons, with an area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUROC) of 79-93%. The estimators correctly predicted larger epidemics in 67-83% of cases and smaller epidemics in 90-100% of cases, with an AUROC of 83-92%. Performance varied by estimator and influenza subtype, with wide uncertainty intervals for some estimates indicating modest precision. In the longitudinal Hong Kong cohort, all four estimators negatively correlated with subsequent 30-day cumulative incidence for H1N1 (Pearson correlations -0·23 to -0·46), whereas H3N2 correlations were mostly non-significant. INTERPRETATION: We developed and validated four complementary population immunity estimators derived from individual antibody titres that showed predictive utility for influenza subtype dominance and epidemic size direction across diverse settings. These estimators could inform seasonal influenza preparedness, surveillance prioritisation, and health-care resource planning. FUNDING: Hong Kong Health Bureau and Research Grants Council.
