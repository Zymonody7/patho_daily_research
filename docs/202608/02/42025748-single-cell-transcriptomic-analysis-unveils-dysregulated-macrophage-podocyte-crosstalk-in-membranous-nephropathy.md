## 摘要
背景：膜性肾病（MN）是一种抗体介导的肾小球疾病，但驱动损伤的细胞网络仍不明确。方法：我们将阳离子牛血清白蛋白（cBSA）诱导的小鼠 MN 模型与单细胞 RNA 测序（scRNA-seq）相结合，以分析肾脏细胞图谱。生物信息学分析包括聚类、差异表达、通路富集和细胞间通讯推断（CellChat）。结果：我们成功建立了表现出典型病理特征（包括肾小球基底膜增厚和 IgG 沉积）的 cBSA 诱导 MN 小鼠模型。单细胞转录组学揭示了 MN 中深刻重塑的肾脏细胞图谱，其特征是免疫激活（巨噬细胞、B 细胞、T 细胞和 NK 细胞增加）以及伴随的肾小管损伤（近端小管细胞显著减少）。我们发现了一种失衡的适应性免疫反应：滤泡辅助性 T（Tfh）细胞扩增，但浆细胞减少，提示分化受阻。通路分析表明 B 细胞中存在代谢重编程，浆细胞中存在内质网（ER）应激，巨噬细胞中存在促炎激活。关键的是，细胞间通讯分析揭示了一个重构的致病性巨噬细胞-足细胞轴。该轴由双重失衡定义：促损伤信号（Spp1-整合素）的功能获得，以及稳态信号（IV 型胶原）的功能缺失。结论：本研究提供了 MN 的全面细胞图谱，并描绘了失调的巨噬细胞-足细胞通讯是肾小球损伤的关键驱动因素，强调了该轴作为治疗干预的潜在靶点。

## Abstract
BACKGROUND: Membranous nephropathy (MN) is an antibody-mediated glomerular disease, but the cellular networks driving injury remain poorly defined. METHODS: We integrated the cationic bovine serum albumin (cBSA)-induced mouse MN model with single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) to profile renal cells. Bioinformatic analyses included clustering, differential expression, pathway enrichment, and cell-cell communication inference (CellChat). RESULTS: We successfully established a cBSA-induced MN mouse model exhibiting characteristic pathological features, including glomerular basement membrane thickening and IgG deposition. Single-cell transcriptomics revealed a profoundly remodeled renal cellular landscape in MN, characterized by immune activation (increases in macrophages, B cells, T cells, and NK cells) and concomitant tubular injury (significant loss of proximal tubule cells). We identified an imbalanced adaptive immune response: T follicular helper (Tfh) cells expanded, but plasma cells decreased, suggesting disrupted differentiation. Pathway analysis indicated metabolic reprogramming in B cells, ER stress in plasma cells, and pro-inflammatory activation in macrophages. Crucially, cell-cell communication analysis uncovered a rewired pathogenic macrophage-podocyte axis. This axis was defined by a dual imbalance: a gain-of-function in injury-promoting signals (Spp1-integrin) coupled with a loss-of-function in homeostatic signals (collagen IV). CONCLUSION: This study provides a comprehensive cellular atlas of MN and delineates a dysregulated macrophage-podocyte crosstalk as a key driver of glomerular injury, highlighting this axis as a promising target for therapeutic intervention.
